# One Price — music library
All beds are slow and soothing, public-domain compositions rendered as our own performance (or original). 50 s, faded, mixed for voice on top.
- satie_gymnopedie1 — Satie, Gymnopédie No. 1 (1888). Signature; thesis and grid episodes, reflective struggles.
- pachelbel_canon — Pachelbel, Canon in D (c. 1680). Family and relationship struggles.
- ground_a_minor — original ground bass in A minor, Canon-shaped. Fear, envy, loss.
Assignment per reel is in social/reels_manifest.json ("music").
